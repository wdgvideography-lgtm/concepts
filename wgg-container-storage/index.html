About WGG Container Storage

Serious Storage, Backed by a Serious Name

WGG Container Storage is part of the William Gilder Group — one of Gloucestershire's most established haulage, logistics, and environmental services companies with over 25 years of proven operational expertise.

We offer flexible container storage solutions from our secure, well-managed site — ideal for homeowners, tradespeople, retailers, construction companies, and any business that needs extra space without a long-term commitment.

  • Fully fenced, gated site with 24/7 CCTV coverage
  • Wind and waterproof steel shipping containers
  • Flexible rental terms — weekly, monthly, long-term
  • Drive-up access for easy loading and unloading
  • Domestic and commercial customers welcome
  • Competitive, transparent pricing — no hidden fees
